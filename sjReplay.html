sjReplay

Automatic slow-motion replays for ski-jump training. Capture each take-off and review the movement hands-free on your iPhone.

View in App Store

Train. Take off. Replay.

Place your iPhone where it can see the full movement, choose a camera, and tap Start. sjReplay recognises the ski-jump sitting position and automatically records the attempt. It detects the take-off and loops the clip in slow motion so athletes and coaches can review the movement before the next attempt.

Features

  • 1080p recording at 60 fps
  • Automatic ski-jump position detection
  • Take-off detection to time each replay
  • Replay starts up to 5 seconds before take-off
  • Adjustable recording time after take-off, from 1 to 8 seconds
  • Looping slow-motion playback with adjustable speed
  • Automatically returns to recording for the next attempt
  • Save selected replays to Photos

Privacy and support

sjReplay uses the camera to detect the ski-jump position and record training after you tap Start. Video processing takes place on your iPhone. Photos access is requested when you choose to save a replay.
